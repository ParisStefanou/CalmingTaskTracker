Privacy, plainly stated.

Privacy policy · Last updated October 19, 2026

Everything you enter stays on your device. CalmingTaskTracker has no account system, advertising, analytics or tracking, and it does not connect to the internet.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the tasks you enter (title, description, time needed, due date, importance, repeat and progress), a history of changes to your tasks so they can be undone, and your settings. This information is processed on your device and is never sent to the developer or anyone else.

Android may back up app data to your Google account according to your device backup settings; Google handles that backup under its own policies.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to remind you when a task has no time to spare. You can turn reminders off in the app's Settings or in Android's settings.
  • Run at startup: to set your reminders again after the phone restarts.
  • Display over other apps: only for the floating timer, and only after you start it.

Retention and deletion

Your data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. History entries can be deleted inside the app, and clearing the app's storage in Android's settings removes everything. Deleting app data does not remove data already included in your platform backup.

Children

CalmingTaskTracker is not directed at children under 13. The developer does not collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

Any changes will be published on this page with an updated date.
